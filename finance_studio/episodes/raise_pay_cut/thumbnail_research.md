# Thumbnail & title research - Episode 1 (2026-11-23)

## 1. Competitor scan (YouTube US search, 4 queries, ~30 videos)
Queries: "why your raise feels like a pay cut", "real wages inflation explained", "why do i feel poor inflation", "money illusion inflation raise".

**The exact title is saturated.** At least 10 small channels used "Why Your Raise Feels Like a Pay Cut" or a near-copy recently
(e.g. 3, 6, 33, 73, 136 views). Their thumbnails look alike: cartoon person + "RAISE / PAY CUT?" + gold coins + red arrow.

**What won the topic:**
| Video | Views | Thumbnail pattern |
|---|---|---|
| How Money Works - "How Is Everything Outpacing Inflation At The Same Time?" | 1.56M | Data chart: cost lines outrunning an "INFLATION" line + 4-word hand-drawn headline |
| CNBC - "Why Americans Feel So Poor" | 3.6M | One emotional object (empty wallet) + 4 words |
| Humphrey Yang - "Why You Don't Feel Rich (Even When You Are)" | 194K | One worried face, no text |
| CNBC - "Why Salaries In The U.S. Don't Keep Up With Inflation" | 221K | Photo + 6 words |

Lesson: one strong idea (a contradiction or an emotion), few words, and frame it around **feeling poor**, not "pay cut".

## 2. Evidence (full brief with links in the research agent report)
- YouTube Test & Compare picks winners by **watch-time share**, not CTR (support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400).
- High CTR + low view duration is treated as clickbait and is less likely to be recommended (answer/7628154).
- Eden (507k videos) and vidIQ (500 breakouts, 2026): short text (about 4-6 words) slightly favored; **shocked faces, brightness and
  saturation show no measurable lift**; one arrow/circle has a small edge; illustration is not penalized.
- Spec: 16:9, 3840x2160 recommended (1280x720 still valid), under 2 MB for mobile upload.
- **Thumbnails are not a search-ranking factor** per YouTube's "How search works" page; ranking uses title, description, tags,
  content relevance and engagement. No evidence YouTube reads thumbnail text. SEO happens in the title and description.
- Policy: every thumbnail claim must be delivered in the video (egregious-clickbait enforcement, spam policy).

## 4. Title recommendation (the actual SEO lever)
Avoid the saturated exact phrase. Pair the hero thumbnail with a title that adds information rather than repeating the text:
1. **Why You Feel Poorer Even After a Raise** (recommended)
2. Why Americans Earn More but Feel Broke
3. Record Income, Shrinking Paychecks: The Raise Paradox Explained

Search volume was not measured: vidIQ has 0 credits until Dec 13. Re-check keyword volume then, before locking the title.
Put "raise", "inflation", "real wages" and "feel poor" in the first two lines of the description.
