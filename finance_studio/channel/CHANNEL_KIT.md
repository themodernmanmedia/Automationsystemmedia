## 5. Per-video SEO template (follow on every upload)
What YouTube's own Help Center says matters: the **title, thumbnail and description** (the first lines show before "more").
**Tags play a minimal role** and are mainly for misspellings and variants.

- **Title (100 characters max):** a question or contrast that includes one main autocomplete phrase. No "get rich quick" promises.
- **Description:** the main keyword in the first 2 lines, then chapters (first at **00:00**, at least 3, each **at least 10 s**), sources, the fiction note and the disclaimer.
- **Hashtags:** 3, which show above the title. Over 60 means all of them are ignored.
- **Tags:** 5-10 variants and misspellings, 500-character limit.
- **Thumbnail:** one contrast, 4-6 words, one arrow, nothing in the bottom-right corner; run Test & Compare with 3 variants.
- **Shorts:** 3-5 vertical clips per episode, each linked to the full video through *Related video*.

## 7. Launch checklist
1. Create the channel as a **Brand Account** named "Cents Unseen" and claim **@centsunseen**. Name and handle can each change only twice in 14 days, so decide first.
2. Paste the About text (section 2) and the channel keywords (section 3); set the country to United States.
3. Upload the banner and avatar. I can generate them from the Maya and Dev artwork in the channel style.
4. Upload Episode 1 with the metadata in section 6, and run Test & Compare on the 3 thumbnails.
5. Post 3-5 Shorts from Episode 1 over the following week, each linking to the full video.
6. Optional: register centsunseen.com and grab the same handle on TikTok, Instagram and Facebook.
7. Publish on a schedule you can keep (the original brief: 2 long videos a week). YouTube rewards consistency but doesn't penalize breaks.
8. Monetization (YouTube Partner Program) needs 1,000 subscribers plus 4,000 watch hours. **From Feb 1, 2027 new applicants need 8,000 watch hours** (or 20M Shorts views).
9. Keep scripts, visuals and stories varied so nothing looks mass-produced, which YouTube's "inauthentic content" monetization policy targets.
10. After 5 videos, use YouTube Studio's Audience and Research tabs to pick the next topics. The algorithm follows your audience.

**Honest note on "viral":** nothing can guarantee it. What gives a video its best chance:
- Packaging that reads at a glance (the thumbnail plus a title built on a real search phrase).
- A first 20 seconds that pays off the promise.
- High retention, because YouTube judges by watch time, not clicks.
- Consistent uploads with Shorts feeding the long videos.
