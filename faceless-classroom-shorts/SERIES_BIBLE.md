# "ROOM 13" — Faceless Animated POV Shorts

An original series that uses the same format that works for @tonka.vrchat:
first-person POV, a cartoon classroom, exaggerated reaction faces, one-line
"rizz"/troll bits, 6–10 second loops. It uses **no** Stranger Things characters,
names or likenesses, and no footage from anyone else.

## Why the format works (from the research)
- 96 recent Shorts average about 660K views; the top one has 3.2M.
- The hook lands in the first second: a character's face fills the frame from the viewer's POV.
- One catchphrase per video, and the same titles get reused once a bit works.
- Fixed hashtag set + emoji at the end of every title.
- Almost no editing: a watermark and a single beat.

**Setting anchor:** Stylized 3D animated look like a cozy indie video game: soft toon shading, big expressive cartoon faces, warm afternoon sunlight through classroom windows, chalkboard and rows of desks behind. The viewer's cartoon hands are on a wooden desk at the bottom of the frame.

**Watermark:** "@ROOM13" bottom-center (add in the edit, not in the prompt).

## Title formula

## Production pipeline (per Short)
1. Pick a script below (each one = one 8-second clip).
2. Render with **Veo 3.1 Fast** on ElevenLabs at 9:16. It generates the dialogue, sound effects and laughter in the same clip. Cost: about **7.3K ElevenLabs credits (~$1.32)** per clip. Full-quality Veo 3.1 costs about 19.4K (~$3.53).
   - Writing "9:16" in the prompt does **not** set the aspect ratio. The pilot came out 16:9. Set Aspect ratio = 9:16 on the video node in the ElevenLabs flow before running, or reframe afterwards with `make_vertical.sh`.
3. Optional: loop the clip, add the watermark + burned captions (CapCut or ffmpeg).
4. Post 1–3 a day. Reuse a title for each new take on a bit that hits 2× your average.

Budget: the current ElevenLabs balance (about 123K credits) covers **about 16 clips** on Veo Fast.

## Scripts (8s each — paste after the cast + setting anchors)

1. **Pencil rizz** — PIP leans into the camera: "You got a pencil? 'Cause you just drew my attention." TANK screams, grabs his hood: "PIP. CHILL. BRO." and drags him off, still doing finger-guns.
2. **Frog ambush** — Quiet classroom. MOMO rises slowly from under the desk right in front of the camera, frog-hood eyes first, and whispers "ribbit." Viewer's hands jump; TANK in the background screams.
3. **Aura farm** — DEX walks past in slow motion, wind blowing his hair indoors, flips his earbud out: "GG. Too easy." He trips over a backpack. Class laughs.
4. **Teacher reveal** — PIP: "Ms. Bean ain't even here, watch this—" stands on a desk to dance. The camera pans to MS. BEAN sipping from the "NO" mug, staring. PIP freezes. SFX: record scratch.
5. **Kawaii overload** — MOMO squishes the camera's cheeks with both hands: "You're so kawaii I'm gonna throw up!" TANK: "MOMO NOOO."
6. **Homework steal** — TANK slowly slides the viewer's paper away while maintaining eye contact, mouths "mine." The viewer's hand slaps it back. Dramatic orchestral sting.
7. **Rizz rehearsal** — PIP practicing on the camera: "Are you a fire drill? 'Cause—" DEX: "Bro it's a fire alarm." PIP: "…'cause you're ALARMING." Everyone groans.
8. **Seat taken** — POV sitting down; DEX, PIP, TANK and MOMO all lean in at once, faces filling the frame, and say together, "That's our seat." Freeze-frame scream faces.
9. **Lunch trade** — MOMO offers a single grape: "Trade for your whole pizza?" Viewer's hand nods yes. TANK, horrified: "BRO GOT SCAMMED."
10. **Sub teacher** — The door opens; a substitute who is just TANK in a fake mustache: "I'm… Mr. Tonk. Homework is cancelled." Class cheers. MS. BEAN appears in the doorway behind him.
